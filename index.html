Keep remodel tasks, site records, evidence and punch-list follow-up in one private workspace.

Getting started

On first launch, add your own property or open the sample project to explore tasks, site records, issue follow-up and PDF reports. Manage or switch between properties from Archive > Manage Properties.

Project templates

Choose a project location and project type to receive relevant suggested checkpoints. Every checkpoint is optional. Regional prompts are planning reminders only; confirm permit, inspection and licensing requirements with local authorities and qualified professionals.

Photos and reminders

Photos are always optional. You can continue with text records if the camera is unavailable or permission is denied. Due-date reminders can be enabled or disabled in Archive.

PDF reports and project backup

Archive can generate a paginated PDF with selected site photos for sharing through the iOS share sheet. Project Backup exports a .renotrace file containing the current project's tasks, issues, history, records, changes and photos. Restoring a backup adds a new property and does not overwrite existing projects.

Data storage

No account is required. Project data is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded by the app. Before deleting the app, export any project backups and reports you need. Deleting the app removes its local project data.

Contact support

Email peisuu@outlook.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, the screen where the issue occurred and steps to reproduce it. Do not send site photos containing an address or other sensitive information.

开始使用

首次打开 App 后，可以添加自己的房屋，也可以先进入示例项目体验任务、现场记录、问题整改和 PDF 报告。多个房屋可在“档案 > 管理房屋”中切换。

项目模板

选择项目所在地和项目类型后，App 会提供对应的建议检查任务。每项任务都可单独选择。地区提示只用于项目准备，请向当地主管机构和有资质的专业人员确认审批、检查与持证要求。

照片和提醒

现场照片始终是可选项。相机不可用或未授权时，可以继续使用文字记录。到期提醒可在“档案”中开启或关闭。

PDF 报告和项目备份

“档案”可生成包含所选现场照片的分页 PDF，并通过 iOS 系统分享。“项目备份”会导出包含任务、问题历史、现场记录、变更和照片的 .renotrace 文件。恢复时会加入一个新房屋，不会覆盖已有项目。

数据保存

当前版本无需账号，项目保存在设备本地，App 不会主动上传。删除 App 前，请先导出需要保留的项目备份和报告；删除 App 会移除设备上的本地项目数据。

联系支持

遇到问题时，请发送邮件至 peisuu@outlook.com，并附上 iPhone 型号、iOS 版本、问题出现的页面和复现步骤。请不要发送包含住址或其他敏感信息的现场照片。
